## Live observation and verification

Actual GPU browser observations showed advancing simulation time at 200 Hz
(40.3 to 41.5 s), 400 Hz (12.6 to 13.4 s) and 800 Hz (38.8 to 39.3 s), with one
3D canvas and zero page errors. The final 800 Hz session changed to
`Completed · recording verified` only after the parent verified its recordings.
Screenshots and raw worker logs stay local; the workflow reproduces the monitor.

82 Python checks, two native CTest checks, 11 frontend contract checks and three
legacy viewer checks passed locally. Nine browser checks passed; three checks
for other selected replay scenarios were inapplicable. The additional symbolic
link test could not create links under the Windows host policy and was skipped
locally; it is included in the Linux CI suite. HTTP tests verify Host/Origin,
path and method restrictions, bounded sanitization, stale data and no caching.
Browser fixtures verify bad packets, reconnect, failure/completion labels,
bounded history, 3D disposal and layout. Fixtures are not physics evidence.

The project evidence index, public-source privacy scan and UTF-8 review passed.
The full-rate verifier checks source/configuration hashes, exact seeded wind,
control recurrence, rotor allocation/lag and original mission outcomes before
the comparison tool emits the public summary.

## Development and remaining limits

Development seed 73 completed the 50 s turbulent mission at 400 Hz physics with
unchanged 200 Hz control, wind and actuator cadence. All mission gates passed:
position RMSE 0.083518 m, peak error 0.256850 m, peak tilt 7.582221 degrees,
landed at 43.470 s. Wall-paced execution took 68.744 s in the control loop
(approximately 0.727x real-time factor), with maximum lag 18.744 s. This development run
used a dirty revision and is not final clean-source acceptance evidence.

The three-seed study is limited to the declared route and temporal wind model.
State feedback remains perfect; drag, rotors and contact are illustrative and
uncalibrated. No IMU/estimator noise or delay, battery model, ground effect or
hardware timing guarantee is claimed. The next physics investigation remains
constant-spin/torque and pose-sampling isolation of the independent yaw finding.
